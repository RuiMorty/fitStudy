## 计数与解剖参考入口

核心206采用常用成人骨骼教学计数：中轴80、附肢126。以下为后续逐结构和关节校准的参考入口，**本轮未逐页在线核对所有条目，也未完成专家审核**。目前清单是可审查的计划，不宣称逐骨医学准确。

| 数据参考ID | 参考入口 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| COUNT_ADULT | [OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e 7.1](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-1-divisions-of-the-skeletal-system) | 成人中轴/附肢骨分类与计数 |
| AXIAL_SKULL | [7.2 The Skull](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-2-the-skull) | 颅骨、听小骨与舌骨的分类参考 |
| AXIAL_SPINE | [7.3 The Vertebral Column](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-3-the-vertebral-column) | 脊柱与成人融合计数 |
| AXIAL_THORAX | [7.4 The Thoracic Cage](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-4-the-thoracic-cage) | 胸骨、肋骨、肋软骨关系 |
| APPENDICULAR_UPPER | [8.1 The Pectoral Girdle](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-1-the-pectoral-girdle)、[8.2 Bones of the Upper Limb](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb) | 肩带和上肢分区 |
| APPENDICULAR_LOWER | [8.3 The Pelvic Girdle and Pelvis](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-3-the-pelvic-girdle-and-pelvis)、[8.4 Bones of the Lower Limb](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-4-bones-of-the-lower-limb) | 骨盆带和下肢分区 |
| JOINTS | [9.4 Synovial Joints](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/9-4-synovial-joints)、[9.6 Anatomy of Selected Synovial Joints](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/9-6-anatomy-of-selected-synovial-joints) | 关节类型、稳定结构与运动基础 |

运动轨迹、活动范围、肌肉附着足印、籽骨及肌腱滑动不能只凭上述概览决定。进入具体部位制作前，要增加与该关节/组织直接相关的解剖图谱、解剖或影像研究，记录文献页码/图号、样本人群、姿势、主动/被动条件及测量约定。
