The current international standard for human anatomical terminology is based on the Terminologia Anatomica 1 (TA), developed by the Federative Committee on Anatomical Terminology (FCAT) and the International Federation of Associations of Anatomists (IFAA). The Terminologia Anatomica contains the terminology of around 7,500 anatomical (macroscopic) structures. It was created to standardize the language of anatomy.
