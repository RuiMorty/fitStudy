Find sources: "Radiate carpal ligament" – news · newspapers · books · scholar · JSTOR ( April 2026 ) ( Learn how and when to remove this message ) Radiate carpal ligament Details From Capitate To Triquetral , lunate , scaphoid Identifiers Latin ligamentum carpi radiatum TA98 A03.5.11.103 TA2 1805 FMA 42298 Anatomical terminology [ edit on Wikidata ] The radiate carpal ligament is a group of about seven fibrous bands which diverge in all directions on the palmar surface of the carpal bones.
