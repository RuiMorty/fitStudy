radius, lunate and capitate head of capitate acts as center of rotation proximal row (lunate) acts as a unit and is an intercalated segment with no direct tendon attachments distal row functions as unit advantage efficient motion (less motion at each link) strong volar ligaments enhance stability disadvantage more links increases instability of the chain scaphoid bridges both carpal rows login
